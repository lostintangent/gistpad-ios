# Learning to Bake Bread

I started baking bread last winter, mostly because the house felt too quiet. What began as a way to fill a Sunday afternoon turned into a weekly habit.

Things changed when I started keeping a notebook. I wrote down the flour, the water temperature, how long the dough rose and how the crumb looked. After a month the patterns was obvious, and my loafs started to rise like they was supposed to.

Now I bake two loaves every Saturday. One stays with us and the other goes to whoever on our street needs it most that week.
