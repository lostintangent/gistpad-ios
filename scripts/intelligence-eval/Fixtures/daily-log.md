## Journal

At lunch I took the long way around the park. The gravel path was muddy, and the small bridge
was closed, so I turned back at the reeds. It was still worth getting outside for twenty
minutes. I listened to an interview about how people learn to draw by noticing the spaces
between objects. That idea stuck with me during the afternoon review: the empty space around
a control can be as useful as the label on it.

I had a brief call with my sister after work. She is planning a weekend visit but hasn't
chosen a train yet. We talked about making soup and walking to the old bookstore if the
weather holds. I have not booked tickets or a table; I should not write either as a fact.
She asked about the small fern in the window, and I admitted that its leaves have been
drooping. Tomorrow I'll check the pot before deciding it needs more water.

The best part of the day was the ordinary half hour after dinner. We cleared the table,
played one record, and did not hurry into another screen. I want to remember that feeling
more than the exact order of tasks. A daily note helps me notice which plans are real and
which are just ways to feel busy. I can leave the chapter unread tonight and still have
had a good day.
